Pad KO

Boxing pads on your iPhone

Pad KO puts pads beside your head so you can train with the front camera.

Train

Free Play lets you set the mix, speed, pad size, and round length. Drills and programs give you combinations to repeat. Trophy Road is a series of opponents. Meta is a short uppercut, jab, and hook round.

On your phone

Get AI feedback on your shadowboxing in Free Play. The scores are calculated on your iPhone from joint positions. Camera video is not recorded or uploaded. The scores are an estimate, not a coach and not medical advice.

Pad KO does not create an account. Your rounds stay on your phone.
