Privacy Policy

Pad KO · Updated November 5, 2026

Pad KO is a boxing training app for iPhone. This policy says what the app does with information on your phone.

Camera

The front camera is used to place pads and detect punches during the onboarding tryout, Free Play, drills, and other rounds. Apple Vision reads each camera frame on this phone and returns joint positions. The app then discards the frame. It does not record camera video, save photos, or upload camera frames or joint positions.

Face data

During a round, Apple Vision body pose runs on this phone. It returns the positions of the nose, eyes, and ears, along with the shoulders, elbows, wrists, hips, knees, and ankles. These are points in the current camera frame. They are not a photo of the face, a face scan, or a way to identify a person.

Pad KO uses those points only to place the pads and to tell when the head moves. The camera frame is discarded as soon as the points are read. The points stay in memory for that frame only. They are not saved on the phone, not recorded as video, and not uploaded.

Face data is not shared with any third party, including Google. It is not sent to a server. After a scored round, the app may send the round counts only — pads hit, pads missed, longest combo, and time — to Google Gemini to write a short recap. Those counts are not face data, and the camera frame and body points are not included.

Face data is not retained. It is dropped when the frame is finished. Reset local data and deleting the app do not delete face data, because face data is never stored. Round scores saved on the phone are workout numbers, not face positions. This section explains how Pad KO collects, uses, discloses, shares, and retains face data.

Saved on this phone

The app saves finished rounds, program progress, personal bests, your display name, onboarding answers, stance, sound, haptics, and pad speed on this phone so the app can show them to you. Leaderboards in the app are the scores saved on this phone. This information stays on the phone until you use Reset local data in Settings or delete the app.

The app does not create an account. It does not collect your location, contacts, health records, or advertising identifier. It does not sell information and it does not use information for advertising.

Skill ratings and AI

Skill ratings start from your onboarding try and update after later scored rounds. Pad hits, misses, reaction times, and on-phone habit scores (guard, punch reach, head movement) are calculated on this phone from the joint positions already used for the pads. They are sample ratings, not a coach and not a medical grade.

After a scored try, the app can send those recorded numbers—not camera frames or joint positions—to Google Gemini to write the breakdown. Camera frames stay on this phone. Reset local data deletes the skill record.

Subscriptions

If you buy Pad KO Monthly or Pad KO Annual, Apple charges your Apple ID. Pad KO does not receive your payment card number. Apple is the only company that receives the purchase. A subscription renews until you cancel it in Settings, then Apple ID, then Subscriptions.

Deleting information

Settings includes Reset local data. That deletes the information the app saved on this phone. It does not cancel an App Store subscription.

Children

Pad KO is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect information from children.
