Privacy Policy

Pad KO · Updated October 14, 2026

Pad KO is a boxing training app for iPhone. This policy says what the app does with information on your phone.

Pad KO uses the front camera to see your body during a session. Apple Vision reads each camera frame on this phone and returns joint positions. The app then discards the frame. It does not record camera video and it does not upload camera frames or joint positions.

Finished rounds, program progress, personal bests, your display name, onboarding answers, stance, sound, haptics, and pad speed are saved on this phone. Leaderboards in the app are the scores saved on this phone. The app does not create an account and does not sell this information.

Record and analyze in Free Play scores guard, stance, punch reach, head movement, and the time between hits on this phone from the joint positions already used for the pads. A normal round is not scored this way. Those scores stay on this phone.

If you buy a subscription, Apple charges your Apple ID. Pad KO does not receive your payment card number. A subscription renews until you cancel it in Settings, then Apple ID, then Subscriptions.

Settings includes Reset local data. That deletes the information the app saved on this phone. It does not cancel an App Store subscription.

Pad KO is not directed at children under 13.

Questions about this policy go to support@padboxing.app.
