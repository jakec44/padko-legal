Privacy Policy

Pad KO · Updated October 14, 2026

Pad KO is a boxing training app for iPhone. This policy says what the app does with information on your phone.

Camera

The front camera is used to place pads and detect punches. Apple Vision reads each camera frame on this phone and returns joint positions. The app then discards the frame. It does not record camera video, save photos, or upload camera frames or joint positions.

Saved on this phone

The app saves finished rounds, program progress, personal bests, your display name, onboarding answers, stance, sound, haptics, and pad speed on this phone so the app can show them to you. Leaderboards in the app are the scores saved on this phone. This information stays on the phone until you use Reset local data in Settings or delete the app.

The app does not create an account. It does not collect your location, contacts, health records, or advertising identifier. It does not sell information and it does not use information for advertising.

Record and analyze

Record and analyze in Free Play scores guard, stance, punch reach, head movement, and the time between hits on this phone from the joint positions already used for the pads. A normal round is not scored this way. Those scores stay on this phone.

Subscriptions

If you buy Pad KO Monthly or Pad KO Annual, Apple charges your Apple ID. Pad KO does not receive your payment card number. Apple is the only company that receives the purchase. A subscription renews until you cancel it in Settings, then Apple ID, then Subscriptions.

Deleting information

Settings includes Reset local data. That deletes the information the app saved on this phone. It does not cancel an App Store subscription.

Children

Pad KO is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect information from children.

Contact

Questions about this policy go to support@padboxing.app.
